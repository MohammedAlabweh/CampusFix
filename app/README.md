# Student app

Flutter mobile app for students (Android and iOS).

MVP screens:

- Sign in with student number (profile holds hall, flat and room)
- Choose where: My flat or Somewhere on campus
- Campus location: scan room QR code, or pick building, floor and room
- Describe the problem and add an optional photo
- Review the AI's category, priority and location, then submit
- Emergency screen with the UWE emergency number
- My reports list and report detail with status timeline

Development starts in phase 4 (January 2027).
